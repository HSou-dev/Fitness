Install NutriLog

Add NutriLog to your Home Screen for a full-screen app experience — no App Store needed.

1
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of Safari, tap the box-with-arrow icon (⎋).
2
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the Share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
3
Tap "Add"
Confirm the name and tap Add in the top-right corner.
💡 Safari only — this only works in Safari. If you're in Chrome or another browser, open this page in Safari first.

Install NutriLog as an app for instant home-screen access.

1
Open the Chrome menu
Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2
Tap "Add to Home screen"
Select Add to Home screen or Install app from the menu.
3
Tap "Install"
Confirm the prompt. NutriLog will appear on your home screen as a full app.
💡 Chrome required — use Chrome or Edge. Firefox does not support PWA installation.
